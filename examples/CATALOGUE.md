# BitGames catalogue

The twelve original games, preserved after the generated batch was retired. Quality and children's enjoyment guide future additions; there is no catalogue size target.

| Game | Ages | Learning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [🎈 Balloon Pop](./balloon-pop/public/index.html) | 2–6 | Counting to 10, colours, shapes, adding on and matching dots to quantities |
| [🦆 Bumper Ducks](./bumper-ducks/public/index.html) | 3–8 | Counting in tens (place value), sorting by colour, cooperation and spatial awareness |
| [🐰 Bunny Hop](./bunny-hop/public/index.html) | 3–8 | Counting, comparing and adding, reading and making patterns, and timing |
| [🎂 Cake Stack](./cake-stack/public/index.html) | 3–8 | Sequencing, patterns, counting and equal sharing |
| [🏎️ Crash Racers](./crash-racers/public/index.html) | 5–8 | Map reading, matching and route planning |
| [🐉 Dragon Glide](./dragon-glide/public/index.html) | 4–8 | Number order, counting to 10, sorting by colour and shape, and steering |
| [🎣 Fish Pond](./fish-pond/public/index.html) | 2–8 | Observation, animal habitats (fresh water and sea) and counting |
| [🧩 Memory Match](./memory-match/public/index.html) | 2–8 | Visual and auditory memory, looking closely, animal names and the sounds they make, baby animal words (and which parent each belongs to), and sorting animals by where they live (including that some fit more than one home) |
| [🎨 Paint Splash](./paint-splash/public/index.html) | 2–8 | Colour mixing with primaries (secondary colours and brown), reasoning from a rule to a colour (including two conditions and elimination), planning in coats while the paint is wet, staying inside the lines, and cooperation |
| [🐧 Penguin Bowling](./penguin-bowling/public/index.html) | 4–8 | Number bonds to 10, counting, predictions and spatial reasoning |
| [🚀 Rocket Garage](./rocket-garage/public/index.html) | 4–8 | Design comparisons, predictions, experimentation and planning with two variables |
| [⭐ Star Catcher](./star-catcher/public/index.html) | 3–8 | Number order, counting on and back, skip counting, missing numbers in a sequence, place value with tens, number patterns and two-digit jumps, reading two-digit numbers, real constellations and star patterns |
