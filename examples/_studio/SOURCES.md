# Habitat cues

Fish Pond's collection book labels its mixed animal world as pretend. Its specific real-habitat cues were checked against:

- [NOAA Fisheries: Narwhal](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/narwhal): Arctic Ocean habitat.
- [Florida Museum: Orange clownfish](https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/discover-fish/species-profiles/orange-clownfish/): tropical marine reefs and sea-anemone associations.
- [USGS: Habitat Suitability Index Models — Rainbow trout](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/habitat-suitability-index-models-rainbow-trout): freshwater river and lake habitat.

The book does not infer a species or habitat from a generic toy's appearance. Its rainbow-trout note allows sea-run forms rather than implying that every rainbow trout stays in freshwater.
