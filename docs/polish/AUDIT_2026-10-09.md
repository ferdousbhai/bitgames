# Originals audit and polish plan (2026-10-09)

Four read-only reviewers played all twelve originals in Chromium, in portrait (834×1194) and landscape (1194×834), and read their source. They scored each game against the owner's standard: gentle, focus-building, educational, and engaging through depth rather than hooks. No game logged a functional console error. Scripts and screenshots were kept in session scratch space only, so reproduce from the game source rather than relying on them.

## Pattern

The cores are mostly good, and the noise is layered on top. The same hooks recur in nearly every game:

- infinite pulsing buttons and badges
- confetti showers of 90–360 pieces, camera shake and square-wave fanfares
- combo and streak banners, "+N" popups, score, best score and star grades
- speed ramps, countdowns and hurry timers
- idle nudges
- competitive podiums in games described as cooperative

The learning usually sits in an optional mission or a side studio rather than in the core action.

## Plan

### Phase 1: calm pass (small changes, one owner per game, parallelisable)

Apply to every game:

- **Motion:** remove strobes, flicker and infinite CSS pulses and wiggles. Remove camera shake from non-physical events.
- **Celebrations:** shrink each to one soft moment (a few slow particles plus a gentle chord, played once).
- **Feedback:** drop combo, streak and praise spam, "+N" popups, best-score badges and turn-graded stars. Show what the child made or found instead.
- **Audio:** use sine or triangle tones in place of square and sawtooth fanfares. Lower crash, bang and pop gains, and slow fast music loops.
- **Pacing:** replace endless speed ramps with a constant gentle pace. Remove idle nudges or reduce them to a single quiet glow after 30 s or more.

Bugs and blockers to fix in the same pass:

- Bunny Hop and Star Catcher: add a 🏠 button.
- Crash Racers: add mute and music.
- Dragon Glide: fix the star denominator.
- Fish Pond: correct the habitats.
- Penguin Bowling: clear confetti before the next aim.
- Test harness: default Chromium to the real GPU (`--use-angle=gl`). The current Vulkan default falls back to SwiftShader at 2–5 fps.

### Phase 2: learning inside the core loop (one game at a time, with family play before release)

| Game | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Rocket Garage | Before every launch after a part change, ask "farther, same or shorter?" and show a ghost of the last distance |
| Memory Match | Speak names. Add animal↔sound and animal↔baby matching |
| Cake Stack | The friend orders a picture recipe the child builds. Cut and share the cake equally at the party |
| Penguin Bowling | Earned strikes (bumpers guard, don't aim). Standing pins counted aloud every roll |
| Bunny Hop | Counted carrot rows, rhythmic obstacle patterns to predict, an optional 🐢 pace |
| Fish Pond | 4–5 s patient bite window by default. Freshwater and sea places. One observable fact per catch |
| Dragon Glide | One slow speed. Each world has a purpose (numbered rings in order, or gems sorted by colour or shape). The nest waits for the child |
| Star Catcher | Real constellations lit star by star and named on arrival, one per planet. Stars collected into a visible row instead of points |
| Balloon Pop | Capped pace. Spoken "pop the 4 blue ones" sky with picture slots. Counts to 10 |
| Paint Splash | No timer; finish when done. Overlapping primary rollers mix colours (using `colour-model.js`). Cooperative "Together" result |
| Bumper Ducks | Untimed default. Coloured or numbered bubbles into a shared ten-frame jar. Team total, no medals |
| Crash Racers | Separate, slow "Delivery Town" mode with no bots or laps, stops out of lap order, and picture parcels. Keep racing as an opt-in |

### Phase 3: art (Blender CLI builders)

The new content needs:

- **Fish Pond:** a sea or coral place.
- **Star Catcher:** constellation pieces.
- **Crash Racers:** parcels and houses.
- **Dragon Glide:** numbered rings.
- **Penguin Bowling:** themed pins in the prediction dialog.

Reuse existing kits first.
