## Pattern: calm achieved, now too passive

1. **The game makes the decision for the child.** Several games hand over the answer:
   - Bunny Hop carrot rows always fly to Pip.
   - Cake Stack's wanted layer is the only one that stays and glows.
   - Star Catcher's constellation fills itself in, whichever star is caught.
   - Penguin Bowling waddles to the right spot after "where are they?"
   - Balloon Pop ignores pops past the target, and its dots request accepts any balloon.
   - Crash Racers' houses sit on one loop, so the map is never needed.

   Wrong choices cost nothing and right choices change nothing, so the learning is heard rather than used.
2. **Voice overload.** Trips speak 45–66 lines: every carrot, bubble, pin and ring is counted aloud, with repeated number-bond sentences and doubled goal lines. Counting is the lesson, but past 5 a soft tick and a picture should take over.
3. **No step up for 6–8 year olds.** Most loops are solved in under a minute and then repeat. Examples: Paint Splash finds all three secondaries in about 25 s; Bumper Ducks stops at 3 tens; Balloon Pop stops at 10; Rocket Garage has one variable.
4. **Leftover arcade economies:**
   - Dragon Glide: 💎 HUD, magnet, results tallies.
   - Star Catcher: pink and rainbow stars, gems, magnet.
   - Race modes in Bumper Ducks and Crash Racers: podiums, medals, a 1st-place pill, an 11 s countdown.
   - Infinite pulse and wiggle animations on Rocket Garage buttons (`style.css:221,264`, `workshop.css:25,124`).

**Design rule for the next pass:** give every core action one real, low-stakes decision whose right answer depends on the thing being taught. Then add one harder tier, chosen from how the child is doing, so it isn't a gate.

## Improvements, by game (highest impact first)

**Memory Match (8)**
- Add a category mode for 6–8: animal ↔ home or food, "find the three farm animals".
- Auto-advance the level after a win.
- Speak the goal once.
- Make babies more distinct than size alone.
- Shorten the mismatch hold on the higher levels.

**Rocket Garage (8)**
- Add a second hidden variable, such as tank size → fuel → how far turbo carries, or fins → steering. Later planets then need combinations worked out from the notebook.
- Add one gentle in-flight choice, such as two star lanes.
- Fold the 🧪 workshop into the notebook as "test again".
- After the Alien planet, add mission cards: "reach Mars without wobbling".
- Remove the infinite pulse and wiggle animations.

**Fish Pond (7)**
- Make the cast spot matter: micro-habitats (weeds, log, deep water, anemone) weight the bite.
- The bear asks feature riddles first ("something with eight arms"), with a two-feature tier for older kids.
- "Look closer" becomes tap-to-count arms and stripes.
- Add a sorting page in the book: fresh water, sea, or rubbish.
- Fix the octopus and turtle mismatches.

**Cake Stack (7)**
- All layers slide alike, with no glow or hop, so the child checks the order. Pattern cakes hide the last unit.
- An off-centre cake gently leans, so timing matters, with no loss.
- Sharing becomes real division: 4, 6, 8 or 12 slices among 2–4 guests, sometimes with a remainder "for the baker".
- After friend 8: ABB, AABB and growing patterns.
- Make the recipe studio picture-only, or fold it in.

**Paint Splash (6.5)**
- Faint guide shapes or paint requests ("a green fish") give intent and a picture worth keeping.
- Only the child's own roller names a mix, and robots avoid the child's wet and finished areas.
- An older ladder: ratios (yellow-orange), brown from three, tints.
- Tighter portrait framing.

**Bumper Ducks (6.5)**
- Raise the maths: "make 7 = 5 green + 2 pink", 4 tens, "2 tens and 3 = 23".
- Count aloud only within the first ten.
- Add a visible "next goal ▶" and remove the mission chip.
- Give bonks and dashes a job (free stuck bubbles), and add the frog-order arena.
- Calm Race or put it behind a parent toggle.

**Penguin Bowling (6)**
- A correct side choice only shows a target line; the child aims.
- Ask "where?" only when the remaining pins are lopsided.
- Count aloud only 1–5 standing pins, and say each bond once.
- "Bumpers off" plus "knock down exactly 3" targets for older kids.
- A pictorial results card.

**Bunny Hop (6)**
- Make hopping count: carrot arcs need a timed hop, with no pull, so the tray shows "5 of 6, 1 got away".
- Wrong pattern answers replay the pattern and give a second try. Then a pattern ladder: AB → AAB → ABC → growing.
- A pantry goal ("20 for winter") with a running total.
- Fix the portrait framing and the ask-panel position.
- Count aloud to 5 only.

**Balloon Pop (6)**
- Fix the chord bug.
- Make stopping the skill: pops go into the friend's basket, the child hands it over, and one too many floats back.
- Dots become a real quantity match with larger dot faces.
- Older tier: one more/one less, "which colour has most", teens on two ten-frames, colour mixing.
- Move the request card off the spawn zone.

**Dragon Glide (6)**
- Remove the 💎 HUD, the magnet and the results tallies, or turn gems into the gather material.
- The basket holds exactly N, and an extra gets "one too many — leave it?". Stop counting once the goal is met.
- Rings become a choice: two at once, then count-by-2s or odd-only forks.
- Randomise targets each trip.
- Put the numeral above the ring.

**Crash Racers (6)**
- Parcels show contents, not house signs (🦴 → 🐶, 📖 → library), so matching needs inference.
- Add spurs and junctions so the map matters. Praise only a genuinely short route.
- Say the wrong-house line once.
- A cooperative shared tray for friends.
- Calm Race: no position pill, and fix the overflow.

**Star Catcher (5.5)**
- The child places or connects each caught star onto the glowing spot (numbered dots, then no numbers for older kids).
- Falling stars carry the next number or colour, so catching is a choice.
- A larger goal panel or a ghost outline.
- Cut the magnet, pink and rainbow stars, and gems.
- A larger pool of constellations drawn at random, and "find the one with a belt" in the finale.

## Suggested order

1. Fix the bugs listed above.
2. Thin the voice across all twelve with one shared rule in `speech.js` or each counter: count aloud to 5 (or to 10 in counting-first games), then tick; say each sentence once per round.
3. Do the decision passes on the weakest-depth games first: Star Catcher, Bunny Hop, Balloon Pop, Cake Stack, Penguin Bowling.
4. Add older tiers: Rocket Garage's second variable, Bumper Ducks' bonds, Fish Pond's riddles, Memory Match's categories.
5. Remove the leftover economies in Dragon Glide and Star Catcher, and calm the Race modes.

Green tests and these scores do not show that children enjoy the games. Watch the owner's children play after each pass.
