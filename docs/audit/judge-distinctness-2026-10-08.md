# Independent distinctness audit — 8 October 2026

All 101 current local projects reviewed:12 originals and 89 generated adventures. This is a source-based design judgement, not fresh browser play or a measurement of children having fun. I do not know which first 5–6 the children liked.

Verdicts: **12 retain_candidate, 18 rework, 71 remove_candidate**. All 12 retain candidates are originals; even these require family feedback. The 18 rework entries are concepts worth salvaging through substantial redesign, not permission to ship their current versions.

## Main finding

The expansion largely replaced consequential play with a five-step question/answer container. Changing a model, noun or correct-answer vignette often created a new app without changing what a child can do. Some differences are real: four arithmetic operations are not identical, track inference differs from colour matching, Tangram differs from quarter-turn keys, and radial Snowflake creation differs from binary mirror grids. These distinctions still do not prove the current activities are engaging.

The most defensible exact clone sets are the four route planners, three scales, four picture-pattern gap fillers, four paint mixers, three standard mirror grids, two key turners, two fixed-pad builders, two rhythm sequencers and two pitch quizzes. Stronger originals already subsume new identity-memory and fixed mixing activities.

No generated adventure is independently certified as a retain candidate here. This is deliberately a standalone-game judgement: useful educational content may belong inside one richer game without occupying a separate listing.

## Family comparisons

### collect

firefly-lanterns, orchard-baskets, moon-pebbles, coral-cleanup

Four same counting puzzles. Firefly continuous glow gives meaningful sensory feedback and a calm toddler niche; Coral adds one irrelevant shell. Orchard basket and Moon rocket are destinations/rewards, not new rules. Not clones of moving-target Balloon Pop or real-time Star Catcher.

### match

rainbow-postoffice, shape-locksmith, footprint-detective, shadow-theatre

Four games are NOT exact perceptual clones: colour identity, shape identity, shadow silhouette and animal-track inference demand different observations. All are nevertheless one selection followed by a scripted vignette; strongest salvage is Footprint clue inference, while Shape has the clearest cause/effect reward.

### sort

habitat-hotel, recycling-robots, fruit-veggie-ferry, season-suitcase

Different category knowledge, identical six-item bin-delivery loop. Recycling material vs habitat knowledge matters educationally; bins do not make a world simulation. Season overlaps Weather Wardrobe content but has a sorting rather than single-choice action.

### pattern

butterfly-patterns, bead-bridge, train-carriage-rhythm, constellation-code, reef-patterns

Butterfly, Bead, Train and Reef are exact AB/AAB/ABC gap puzzles with token/art/reward swaps. Constellation changes reasoning to number steps, not a pure reskin. None builds a functioning bridge/train/reef state beyond one solved gap.

### order

sprout-sunflower, breakfast-story, frog-life-lab, butterfly-life-lab

Four stories each contain only four stages reused for every round and level. Biological subjects are different learning content but no new action or progression; five-round sessions repeatedly reconstruct the same sequence. Frog transformation has the strongest potential to become a living simulation.

### compare

dinosaur-size-parade, cloud-number-race, ribbon-tailor, deep-sea-divers

Size, quantity, length and depth are genuinely different perceptual dimensions, but only tap-the-extreme is playable. The advertised parade/race/tailor/divers are absent as systems.

### memory

picnic-pairs, planet-pairs, tool-twins, letter-buddies

Picnic and Planet duplicate identity matching already served by original Memory Match (2–10 pairs, character animation, saved stars/bests). Tool and Letter change the relation to helper/object and case matching: meaningful educational variants, weak rationale for separate apps.

### echo

frog-choir, crystal-cave-echo, robot-dance-code, sleepy-owl-lullaby

Frog, Crystal and Robot use the same four-pad Simon sequence and compose controls; Robot arrows produce brief excursions then return to rest, not persistent programmable travel. Owl has three pads/night mood, no unique sleep mechanism. One sound playground could contain them.

### path

bee-pollen-trail, turtle-treasure-map, rover-rescue, penguin-ice-path

All four use the exact same route generator and arrow queue. Hero/goal/blocker and robot launch change presentation, not route costs, sensing, physics or consequences. Rover is the clearest programming representative; rescue is reaching one square, not rescuing entities.

### trace

snail-spiral, kite-flight-school, letter-trails, number-spark-trails

Letters/numbers materially differ from spirals/waves in taught content and multi-stroke formation. Spiral/wave repeat the same path in five steps. Glyph activities still warrant a unified drawing practice module, not four separate game listings.

### arithmetic

acorn-addition, submarine-subtraction, bakery-bundles, star-share

Do NOT call all four exact clones: subtraction conserves a remainder, division requires equal shares, multiplication uses equal trays, addition combines two stashes. They are four operations inside one manipulative learning lab, each still a prescribed action before a numeric quiz.

### balance

bear-balance, parcel-weights, pebble-equations

Three exact clones: identical target/base ranges, +1/+2/+3 increments and beam response. Acorn/parcel/pebble nouns are not different weight mechanics. One exploratory scales activity at most.

### mix

colour-cauldron, rainbow-smoothies, jellyfish-glow-lab, paint-a-planet

All four exact fixed-recipe clones. Choosing smoothie changes cup art, not ingredients/amounts/recipes. Existing Paint Splash colour studio already offers adjustable mixtures, comparison and mosaic creation plus an actual painting game; all four cannibalize a richer original.

### fraction

pizza-moon, watermelon-picnic, rainbow-quilt

Pizza/Watermelon identical radial slices. Quilt changes area representation to rectangle, educationally useful but no new selection rule. Existing Cake Stack Recipe studio already gives equal sharing a party context; generic fraction drills should be a module there rather than three apps.

### clock

cuckoo-clock-garden, space-station-schedule, bunny-bedtime, bakery-alarm

Cuckoo and Space use same random time matching plus different reward prop. Bunny cycles five routine labels but does not enact a day. Bakery higher levels derive ready time from start+duration: a real reasoning difference, although setting a clock is still the entire play loop.

### mirror

butterfly-mirrors, castle-window-symmetry, robot-reflections, snowflake-studio

Butterfly/Castle/Robot use the same binary grid reflection with cosmetic support geometry/reward. Snowflake is materially distinct: one change propagates to all six arms and free mode generates a design. This is one credible creative-tool seed, not four mirror games.

### rotate

gem-turner, rocket-docking, tangram-turntable

Gem and Rocket use precisely the same asymmetric triangular key; game.shape arrow is not read by rotate(). Tangram has seven classical polygon pieces, selection, rotation and placement: materially distinct puzzle. Its Try matching outline button removes destination-search demand, and every puzzle is the same square.

### measure

giraffe-ruler, bridge-builder, garden-fence, comet-tail-measure

Four add-next-unit counters with automatic placement. Giraffe vertical representation is meaningful but placement is not controlled. Bridge alone turns measured completion into traversable-looking object (scripted boat travel), a salvage opportunity, not evidence of current construction play.

### spell

word-rocket, animal-alphabet, picnic-word-basket

Three games use the same three-letter word-building loop; Animal and Word overlap CAT/PIG/DOG. Picnic has a distinct six-food vocabulary, not different mechanics. Word Rocket has the clearest assembled-crew launch payoff. Consolidate vocabulary into one game if salvaged.

### rhyme

rhyming-river

Unique relational language task within the catalogue; same quiz container does not make it a spelling clone. Frog travel and highlighted endings link success to a visible outcome, but child only chooses one of authored options; current content is six fixed sets.

### choice

weather-wardrobe, kindness-cafe, healthy-plate-party, senses-safari

Weather/Kindness/Food/Senses cover different topics but share four/five rotated questions and scripted correct-answer vignette. Kindness allows multiple caring answers but does not branch socially. These are illustrated quizzes, not adventures. A repeatable Explore object button in Senses is not a sensory world.

### experiment

magnet-discovery, float-boat-lab, sunlight-sprouts

Magnet/Float/Grow have different physical outcome animations and meanings, so not simple cosmetic clones. All outcomes are hardcoded bools across six objects and Test always completes the round. Float/Magnet are strongest simulation seeds; Sunlight especially reduces growth conditions/time to one binary supply.

### pitch

sound-wave-lab, birdsong-tuner

Bird/crystal same extreme-frequency comparison and composer. Crystal wave diagram adds a representation, not a new sound system. Frequencies and actual generated tone are the same. Fold composition/high-low into one musical toy rather than two apps.

### rhythm

drum-beat-builder, rain-drop-rhythm

Both same four-to-six beat/rest toggle sequencer at fixed tempo. Drum squash vs falling drop and 150 vs 560 Hz timbre are cosmetic. Drum has a useful musical toy seed but lacks layered voices/tempo/persistence to support much exploration.

### build

toy-town-builder, castle-block-blueprints

Both same 3/4 fixed pads and four height states, different colours/roof. Free mode is real agency, but limited to preset tower heights; neither is a general block builder. Toy Town can be a seed for one construction sandbox, Castle is a duplicate.

### tenframe

dino-egg-nest, ladybird-dot-party

Both identical ten-slot count selection. Dino adds hatching animation; Ladybird paints spots. These differ in reward, not rule. Counted hatchlings might support pet-care play if rebuilt; current hatchlings carry no state into later rounds.

## Limits and deletion scope

This judge changed only the two audit files. No game, catalogue entry, Cloudflare resource or deployment was deleted. Current local code can differ from deployed versions; URLs in metadata only indicate a recorded deployment. Recommendations must be reconciled with other independent judges and the owner’s child-loved baseline before destructive action. Child assessment, educator assessment and a physical iPad assessment remain separate and unperformed by this review.
